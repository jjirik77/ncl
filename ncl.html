Strategic Roadmap

Vision 2026 - 2029

Focused on four non-negotiable strategic priorities to secure the future of the cybersecurity workforce.

01

High School Outreach

Goal: Establish NCL as the premier solution for school districts seeking to meet cybersecurity education requirements.

  • ▹ Mapping NCL to high school curriculum standards.
  • ▹ Sustainable partnerships with state education departments.
  • ▹ Launching regular high school coaches' professional development.
02

Industry Engagement

Goal: Position NCL as the first destination for employers seeking verified cybersecurity talent.

  • ▹ Systematic integration of Scouting Reports into industry hiring.
  • ▹ Expanding job listings and career opportunities via partnerships.
  • ▹ Formal industry advisory structure for regular feedback.
03

Financial Accessibility

Goal: Eliminate financial obstacles and create sustainable funding models for long-term accessibility.

  • ▹ Establishing comprehensive scholarship and assistance programs.
  • ▹ Increasing participation from underrepresented communities.
  • ▹ Pursuing federal, state, and private grants for student access.
04

Community Building

Goal: Create vibrant, ongoing communities that support career development and peer learning.

  • ▹ Thriving alumni network providing mentorship and guidance.
  • ▹ Multi-season engagement pathways with progressive skills.
  • ▹ Regional events to strengthen local cybersecurity ecosystems.

Competency Framework

The Nine Domains of Excellence

Directly mapped to the NICE Cybersecurity Workforce Framework.

Open Source Intelligence

Track targets, uncover hidden infrastructure, and gather actionable intelligence from public data.

Cryptography

Decode the complex. Identify encryption techniques, break ciphers, and extract secrets.

Log Analysis

Detect malicious patterns in system logs to stop breaches before they spiral.

Network Analysis

Analyze packet captures (PCAPs) to identify malicious traffic and map target topology.

Forensics

Perform post-mortem analysis of disk images to recover files and reconstruct timelines.

Scanning & Recon

Identify live hosts, open ports, and vulnerable services across target networks.

Password Cracking

Recover plaintext passwords from hashes using dictionaries and rulesets.

Web Exploitation

Identify and exploit vulnerabilities in web applications like SQLi and XSS.

Enumeration

Systematically gather info on target systems (users, groups) to identify entry points.
